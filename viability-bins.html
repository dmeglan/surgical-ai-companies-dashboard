Surgical AI viability bins: backing data for the 'Where the 151 companies land' chart
Method: bins are the author's hypotheses about the most probable 2031 outcome, assigned from the master roster (status, funding, notes) combined with public-source research, updated July 29, 2026 after a company-by-company verification pass. Large diversified companies (Intuitive, Medtronic, Stryker, J&J, Olympus, Zeiss, Philips, Brainlab, Accuray, ATEC) are represented as incumbent-platform entries. Bins assess category economics, not team or technology quality; corrections are welcome.
